# FD-01: Breach of Fiduciary Duty (Utah)
Status: Verified 2026-09-21 | Last reviewed by: Claude (AI). Attorney review required.

## When to use / when not to
- **Use** when {{Defendant}} owed {{Plaintiff}} a fiduciary duty because of a recognized relationship (for example, partner or joint venturer, trustee, agent, attorney, corporate officer or director, or a mortgage broker holding a client's funds) or because of a relationship of special trust shown by the facts, and {{Defendant}} breached that duty.
- **Do not use** for an ordinary arm's-length commercial relationship whose duties come only from a contract. The economic loss rule may bar the claim (see Bars).

## Elements
E1. A fiduciary relationship existed. Authority: *Gables at Sterling Vill. Homeowners Ass'n v. Castlewood-Sterling Vill. I, LLC*, 2018 UT 4, ¶ 52, 417 P.3d 95.
E2. {{Defendant}} breached the fiduciary duty. Authority: same.
E3. Causation, both actual and proximate. Authority: same.
E4. Damages. Authority: same (citing *Christensen & Jensen, P.C. v. Barrett & Daines*, 2008 UT 64, 194 P.3d 931).
Sources of duty for partners: loyalty (including accounting for partnership property and profits, not dealing adversely, not competing) and care (refraining from "grossly negligent or reckless conduct, intentional misconduct, or a knowing violation of law"). Utah Code § 48-1d-405(1)–(3).

## Alternative-pleading notes
- Pair with JV-01 (co-venturers), ACC-01, and CONV-01 (misapplied funds). Plead the breach of contract (BOC-01) separately; the fiduciary claim must rest on a duty independent of the contract (see Bars).
- If the fiduciary claim rests on misrepresentation or concealment, Rule 9(c) particularity may apply. *Shah v. Intermountain Healthcare, Inc.*, 2013 UT App 261, ¶ 12, 314 P.3d 1079 (criticizing failure to explain "how the Defendants specifically breached their individual fiduciary duties"). Use the FRD-01 checklist.

## Prerequisites / bars / common defenses
- **Economic loss rule:** tort claims that merely duplicate contract duties are barred; a claim based on "a recognized independent duty of care" is not. *HealthBanc Int'l, LLC v. Synergy Worldwide, Inc.*, 2018 UT 61, ¶¶ 12, 15, 435 P.3d 193. Fiduciary relationships can supply an independent duty (*Gables*, 2018 UT 4, ¶ 51: a limited fiduciary duty "constitutes an independent duty of care" outside the economic loss rule).
- **Defenses:** no fiduciary relationship; consent after full disclosure; business judgment; no causation.
