# CONV-01: Conversion (Utah)
Status: Verified 2026-09-21, except where marked [UNVERIFIED] | Last reviewed by: Claude (AI). Attorney review required.

## When to use / when not to
- **Use** when {{Defendant}} willfully took, kept, sold, or destroyed specific personal property (goods, equipment, certificates, shares, or a specific identified fund) that {{Plaintiff}} was entitled to possess immediately.
- **Money:** Utah has upheld conversion of a specific, identified sum held for a stated purpose. In *Bennett v. Huish*, 2007 UT App 19, ¶¶ 31–33, 155 P.3d 917, a broker agreed to hold part of the loan proceeds for extension fees and return the unused funds; his refusal to return them was conversion. [UNVERIFIED] The general rule that a mere failure to pay a debt, with no specific identifiable fund, is not conversion is widely stated but was not confirmed in Utah authority for this library. Research before pleading conversion of money.
- **Do not use** for a simple failure to pay a contract debt; use BOC-01.

## Elements
E1. {{Plaintiff}} was entitled to immediate possession of the property when the conversion occurred. "Conversion is concerned with possession, not title." Authority: *Fibro Trust, Inc. v. Brahman Fin., Inc.*, 1999 UT 13, ¶ 20, 974 P.2d 288.
E2. {{Defendant}} willfully interfered with the property, without lawful justification. Authority: *Allred v. Hinkley*, 8 Utah 2d 73, 328 P.2d 726, 728 (1958) ("A conversion is an act of wilful interference with a chattel, done without lawful justification by which the person entitled thereto is deprived of its use and possession."); *Fibro Trust*, 1999 UT 13, ¶ 20.
E3. {{Plaintiff}} was deprived of the property's use and possession; {{Defendant}} exercised control inconsistent with {{Plaintiff}}'s right of possession. Authority: same. Conversion requires intentional conduct but "not . . . a conscious wrongdoing." *Allred*, 328 P.2d at 728.
E4. Damages: generally "the full value of the property." *Allred*, 328 P.2d at 728.

## Prayer language
- Judgment for the full value of the Property, {{Amount}}, [[or return of the Property plus damages for its detention (research replevin under Utah R. Civ. P. 64 and following)]], prejudgment interest [[in *Bennett*, prejudgment interest was affirmed where the loss was "fixed" and calculable]], [[punitive damages]], costs, and other just relief.

## Alternative-pleading notes
- Often paired with FD-01 (a fiduciary holding funds) and BOC-01. When the only duty is contractual, expect an economic-loss-rule or "mere debt" challenge (see FD-01 on *HealthBanc*).
- *Fibro Trust* holds that a defendant's legal title "does not necessarily foreclose" a conversion claim where the defendant agreed to hold the property in trust (¶ 21).

## Prerequisites / bars / common defenses
- **Defenses:** consent; {{Plaintiff}} had no right to immediate possession; lawful lien or setoff; the claim is only a contract debt.
