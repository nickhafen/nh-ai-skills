# AS-01: Account Stated (Utah)
Status: Verified 2026-09-21 | Last reviewed by: Claude (AI). Attorney review required.

## When to use / when not to
- **Use** in debt and collection matters when the parties had prior money transactions, {{Plaintiff}} rendered a statement of the balance, and {{Defendant}} agreed to it expressly or impliedly (for example, by paying on it, or by keeping it without objection for a reasonable time [[confirm that Utah recognizes implied assent by silence on these facts]]) and promised, expressly or impliedly, to pay.
- An account stated is a contract. It confirms an existing debt; it does not create liability where none existed before. Pair it with BOC-01, BOC-02, or UE-01 for the underlying debt.
- **Do not use** when the balance was disputed when it was stated, or when there was no prior debt (for example, services performed gratuitously).

## Elements
E1. Previous transactions between the parties gave rise to a debt from one to the other. Authority: *Dementas v. Estate of Tallas*, 764 P.2d 628, 634 (Utah Ct. App. 1988).
E2. The parties agreed on the amount due and that it was correct. Authority: same.
E3. The debtor promised, expressly or impliedly, to pay the creditor the amount owed. Authority: same.
E4. The underlying claim has the basic elements of a contract, including consideration; the rendering of an account "does not create a liability where no liability existed before." Authority: same (quoting *Davis & Cox v. Summa Corp.*, 751 F.2d 1507, 1516 (9th Cir. 1985)).

## Prayer language
- Judgment for {{Amount}}, plus prejudgment interest [[at the agreed rate / at the legal rate under Utah Code § 15-1-1]] from {{Date}}.
- Attorney fees only if a contract or statute provides for them [[identify]], plus costs and other just relief.

## Alternative-pleading notes
- Plead with BOC-01 (the underlying contract). If the contract claim fails for lack of consideration, the account stated claim fails too (*Dementas*, 764 P.2d at 634). It adds value mainly by fixing the amount and making proof easier.
- UE-01 can be pleaded in the alternative for the underlying goods or services.

## Prerequisites / bars / common defenses
- **Statute of frauds:** credit agreements must be in writing (Utah Code § 25-5-4(1)(f)).
- **Defenses:** a timely objection to the statement; lack of an underlying debt; payment; mistake in the account.
