ATTORNEY-CLIENT COMMUNICATION

Dear Ms. Alvarez:

I am writing to update you on the status of your claim against Brightwater Logistics, Inc. and to discuss the settlement proposal we received from Brightwater's counsel last week.

As you know, we filed your complaint in March alleging that Brightwater terminated your employment in retaliation for your reports about the forklift safety issues at the Eastfield warehouse. Since then, the parties have exchanged written discovery, and we took the depositions of your former supervisor and the warehouse operations manager. The Hendricks deposition went better than expected, and in my view it materially strengthens our position on causation, although the documents produced so far are mixed on the question of whether the stated reason for your termination was pretextual.

On September 15, Brightwater's counsel proposed to resolve the case for a payment of $62,500 in exchange for a general release of all claims, a mutual non-disparagement clause, a confidentiality provision covering the terms of the settlement, and a neutral reference. The proposal also includes a no-rehire provision. Brightwater has not admitted any wrongdoing and the agreement would say so.

There are a number of factors to weigh. Litigation is inherently uncertain, and while we believe your claims are strong, a jury could see the facts differently, particularly given the attendance warnings in your personnel file. On the other hand, if we prevail at trial, the potential recovery could be substantially higher than the current offer, possibly including back pay, emotional distress damages, and attorney's fees. Trial is currently set for next August, and between now and then we would need to complete expert discovery and respond to the summary judgment motion that Brightwater has told us it intends to file. It may also be possible to negotiate a higher amount, although Brightwater's counsel characterized this as their best offer.

Under our engagement agreement, our fee is 33% of any recovery, and costs advanced are reimbursed from the recovery. To date, costs advanced total approximately $8,700, primarily for deposition transcripts and the court reporter.

On balance, and taking into account all of the considerations above, my recommendation is that you give serious thought to accepting the offer, or at least to authorizing us to make a counterproposal in the range of $85,000 to $95,000, with the understanding that Brightwater may not move.

Brightwater has asked for a response by October 6. Please let me know how you would like to proceed. I am happy to schedule a call to discuss any questions you may have.

Sincerely,
