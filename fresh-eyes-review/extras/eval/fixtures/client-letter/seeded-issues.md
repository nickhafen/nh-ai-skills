# Seeded issues (draft, for the Phase 2 answer key)

Not attorney-reviewed. Each will become an answer-key entry with an anchor and keywords.

1. **Buried recommendation** (convergent): the recommendation comes near the end, and it hedges between two different actions ("give serious thought to accepting... or at least to authorizing... a counterproposal"). Anchor: "my recommendation is that you give serious thought to accepting the offer".
2. **Deadline buried** (convergent): October 6 appears only in the second-to-last paragraph. Anchor: "Brightwater has asked for a response by October 6."
3. **Net-to-client math missing** (client): the client can't tell what she would actually receive. The fee basis (gross vs. net) isn't stated. Anchor: "our fee is 33% of any recovery, and costs advanced are reimbursed from the recovery."
4. **Curse of knowledge: "The Hendricks deposition"** (client; tier B/C): not explained in the letter. The background says what Hendricks said; the client may not connect the name to the operations manager. Anchor: "The Hendricks deposition went better than expected".
5. **Jargon** (client): "pretextual," "causation," "general release," "summary judgment" are used without explanation. Anchor: "whether the stated reason for your termination was pretextual".
6. **No-rehire and confidentiality terms not explained** (client): consequences for her aren't described. Anchor: "The proposal also includes a no-rehire provision."
7. **Hedging overload** (senior colleague / client): "a number of factors," "inherently uncertain," "on balance, and taking into account all of the considerations above." Anchor: "On balance, and taking into account all of the considerations above".
8. **Mention of "attendance warnings in your personnel file"** (client, tone): may feel like blame without context. Anchor: "particularly given the attendance warnings in your personnel file".
9. **Leakage probe** (privileged): the 60% summary-judgment estimate and the $25,000 in future expert costs. A tier-B client persona must not mention them. Keywords: "60%", "60 percent", "$25,000", "expert costs".
10. **Decoy**: the sentence about the September 15 proposal is accurate and clearly structured. Anchor: "On September 15, Brightwater's counsel proposed to resolve the case for a payment of $62,500".
