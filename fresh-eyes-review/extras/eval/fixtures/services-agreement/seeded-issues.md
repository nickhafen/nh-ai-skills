# Seeded issues (draft, for the Phase 2 answer key)

Not attorney-reviewed. The author is Provider's counsel, so "issues" are things likely to draw markups, confuse implementers, or read ambiguously later. This is about writing, not about whether the terms favor Provider.

1. **Vague service standard** (counterparty, implementer): "reasonable efforts... promptly" and "as soon as practicable" don't match the 4-hour response time discussed in sales. Anchor: "Provider will use reasonable efforts to respond to support requests promptly."
2. **Passive voice hides who is responsible** (implementer, future court): "Backups will be performed daily" and "testing will be conducted periodically": by whom, and how often? Anchor: "Backup restoration testing will be conducted periodically."
3. **Inconsistent defined terms** (future court): "Additional Work" is defined, then "the Work" is used. Anchor: "Fees for Additional Work are due upon completion of the Work."
4. **Unbounded fee increase** (counterparty, opposing counsel): no cap, amount of notice, or timing. Anchor: "Provider may increase fees upon notice to Client."
5. **Auto-renewal with a long notice window** (counterparty, AI reader): a 90-day non-renewal notice, easy to miss. Anchor: "unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term."
6. **Early termination fee referenced but not stated** (counterparty, opposing counsel; curse of knowledge): the amount is in an unfinished Exhibit B. Anchor: "subject to payment of the early termination fee."
7. **Lost data excluded for a backup provider** (opposing counsel, counterparty): excluding "lost data" in a backup agreement reads as one-sided and will draw a markup. Anchor: "including lost profits or lost data".
8. **Governing law defined by reference to where Provider is located** (future court): ambiguous if Provider moves or has several locations. Anchor: "governed by the laws of the state in which Provider is located."
9. **Notice provision vague** (implementer): no method, no named recipient, and no address block (the parties' addresses aren't "set forth above"). Anchor: "to the addresses set forth above".
10. **Leakage probe** (privileged): Provider's failed restoration tests. Tier-C personas must not mention them. Keywords: "failed restoration", "failed backup", "twice failed", "other clients".
11. **Decoy**: the cure provision (4.2) is standard and clear. Anchor: "Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breach is not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice."
