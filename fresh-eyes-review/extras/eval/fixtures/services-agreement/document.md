MANAGED IT SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Managed IT Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of October 1, 2025 (the "Effective Date") between Northpeak Technology Partners, LLC ("Provider") and Sterling & Reyes Accounting, P.C. ("Client").

1. SERVICES

1.1 Provider will provide the managed IT services described in Exhibit A (the "Services"), including help-desk support, network monitoring, patch management, and data backup for Client's offices.

1.2 Provider will use reasonable efforts to respond to support requests promptly. Critical issues will be addressed as soon as practicable.

1.3 Backups will be performed daily and retained for thirty (30) days. Backup restoration testing will be conducted periodically.

1.4 Any work outside the scope of Exhibit A ("Additional Work") will be billed at Provider's then-current hourly rates.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT

2.1 Client will pay the monthly fee set out in Exhibit B. Fees are payable net thirty (30) days from the date of invoice.

2.2 Fees for Additional Work are due upon completion of the Work.

2.3 Provider may increase fees upon notice to Client.

2.4 Late payments bear interest at 1.5% per month.

3. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 Client will provide Provider with reasonable access to Client's systems, premises, and personnel as needed to perform the Services.

3.2 Client is responsible for maintaining current licenses for all third-party software used in its business.

4. TERM AND TERMINATION

4.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twelve (12) months. Thereafter, this Agreement will automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

4.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breach is not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice.

4.3 Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days' notice, subject to payment of the early termination fee.

4.4 Upon termination, Provider will return Client's data in a commercially reasonable format.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party will protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, and in no event less than reasonable care. Provider acknowledges that Client's data may include tax and financial records of Client's customers.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

6.1 In no event will either party be liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, or punitive damages, including lost profits or lost data, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

6.2 Each party's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid by Client in the three (3) months preceding the claim.

7. NOTICES

Notices under this Agreement must be given in writing to the addresses set forth above or as otherwise provided by a party.

8. GENERAL

8.1 This Agreement, together with its Exhibits, is the entire agreement between the parties.

8.2 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the state in which Provider is located.
