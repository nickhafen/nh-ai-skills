## Opening

Dear {{client_name}}:

Thank you for selecting Canyon & Crest LLP to represent you. This letter confirms the terms on which our firm will represent you. Please read it carefully. If it accurately reflects our agreement, sign and return a copy to us.

## Scope of Engagement

You have engaged us to represent you in connection with {{matter_description}}. Our representation is limited to that matter. We have not agreed to represent you in any other matter unless we confirm it in writing.

## Fees and Billing

Our fees for this matter are as follows: {{fee_terms}}. We bill monthly. Each invoice describes the work performed and any costs advanced. Payment is due within 30 days of the invoice date.

## Retainer

Before we begin work, you agree to pay a retainer of {{retainer_amount}}. We will deposit the retainer in our client trust account and apply it to our invoices as they are issued. Any unused portion will be refunded to you at the end of the engagement.

## Client Responsibilities

You agree to give us complete and accurate information, to respond promptly to our requests, and to keep us informed of any change in your contact information or circumstances that may affect this matter.

## Termination of Representation

You may end our representation at any time by written notice. We may withdraw as permitted or required by the Utah Rules of Professional Conduct. If our representation ends, you remain responsible for fees and costs incurred through the date it ends.

## File Retention

At the end of the engagement, we will return any original documents you provided. We will keep our file for five years, after which it may be destroyed without further notice to you.

## Closing

We appreciate the opportunity to work with you. If you have any questions about this letter, please call me before signing it.
