## Scope of Engagement

You have engaged us to represent you in connection with {{matter_description}}. Our work includes negotiating and drafting the transaction documents, advising you on their terms, and coordinating the closing. It does not include tax advice, securities filings, or litigation arising from the transaction unless we agree in writing.

## Identity of Client; Other Parties

We represent only you. We do not represent any other party to the transaction, including any lender, investor, co-owner, or counterparty, even if we communicate with them or prepare documents they sign. Those parties should obtain their own counsel.

## Fees and Billing

Our fees for this matter are as follows: {{fee_terms}}. We bill monthly. Each invoice describes the work performed and any costs advanced, such as filing, recording, and search fees. Payment is due within 30 days of the invoice date. If the transaction does not close, our fees for work performed remain payable.

## Retainer

Before we begin work, you agree to pay a retainer of {{retainer_amount}}. We will deposit the retainer in our client trust account and apply it to our final invoice. Any unused portion will be refunded to you at the end of the engagement.

## Closing and Post-Closing Matters

Our engagement ends when the transaction closes and we deliver the closing documents to you. Post-closing work, such as enforcing covenants, handling purchase-price adjustments, or amending the agreements, requires a new engagement letter.

## File Retention

At the end of the engagement, we will return any original documents you provided and deliver a closing set of the executed transaction documents. We will keep our file for seven years, after which it may be destroyed without further notice to you.
