## Scope of Engagement

You have engaged us to represent you in connection with {{matter_description}}. Our representation covers the case through final judgment in the trial court. It does not include any appeal, collection of a judgment, or any related claim unless we agree in writing.

You were served with the summons and complaint on {{service_date}} by {{service_method}}. Under Utah Rules of Civil Procedure 6(a) and 12(a), we calculate that your answer or other response is due on {{answer_deadline}}. So that we have time to prepare a timely response, please sign and return this letter, with the retainer, no later than {{signing_deadline}}. If we do not receive it by then, we cannot guarantee that we can meet the court's deadline.

## Fees and Billing

Our fees for this matter are as follows: {{fee_terms}}. We bill monthly in increments of one-tenth of an hour. Each invoice describes the work performed and any costs advanced, including filing fees, service fees, deposition transcripts, and expert witness fees. Payment is due within 30 days of the invoice date.

## Retainer

Before we begin work, you agree to pay a retainer of {{retainer_amount}}. We will deposit the retainer in our client trust account and apply it to our invoices as they are issued. If the retainer falls below half of its original amount, you agree to replenish it within 15 days of our request. Any unused portion will be refunded to you at the end of the engagement.

## Preservation of Evidence

You must preserve all documents, emails, text messages, social media posts, and electronic data that relate in any way to this dispute. Do not delete, alter, or discard any of this material, even if it seems unhelpful. Destroying evidence can lead to serious court sanctions.

## No Guarantee of Outcome

Litigation is uncertain. We will give you our honest assessment of your case, but we cannot promise any particular result. Any statement about possible outcomes is our professional opinion only, not a guarantee.

## Client Responsibilities

You agree to give us complete and accurate information, to respond promptly to our requests, to be available for depositions, hearings, and trial, and to keep us informed of any change in your contact information or circumstances that may affect this matter.

## Termination of Representation

You may end our representation at any time by written notice. We may withdraw as permitted or required by the Utah Rules of Professional Conduct, subject to any required approval of the court. If our representation ends, you remain responsible for fees and costs incurred through the date it ends.
