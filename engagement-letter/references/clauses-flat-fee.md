## Scope of Engagement

You have engaged us to represent you in connection with {{matter_description}}. The flat fee covers only the services described in this section. We have not agreed to represent you in any other matter unless we confirm it in writing.

## Fees and Billing

Our fee for this matter is a flat fee: {{fee_terms}}. The flat fee covers our professional time for the services described above, regardless of the hours we spend. Costs such as filing and recording fees are not included and will be billed separately at cost.

## Retainer

Before we begin work, you agree to pay {{retainer_amount}} in advance. We will deposit the advance in our client trust account and withdraw it only as it is earned under the milestones below.

## Flat Fee Milestones and Refunds

The flat fee is earned in stages: one-third when we complete our initial consultation and document review, one-third when we deliver the draft documents, and the balance when the matter is completed. If our representation ends early, we will refund any portion of the fee that has not been earned. A flat fee is not earned solely because it is paid in advance.

## Work Outside the Flat Fee

Work outside the scope described above, including disputes, amendments requested after final documents are delivered, or unexpected complications, is not covered by the flat fee. Before we do any such work, we will tell you and agree in writing on an additional fee.

## Termination of Representation

You may end our representation at any time by written notice. We may withdraw as permitted or required by the Utah Rules of Professional Conduct. If our representation ends, you will owe only the portion of the flat fee earned under the milestones above, plus costs incurred.
